The person behind this site

Hi, I'm Soph.

I'm a product manager. Building things people use is my day job, not a side interest, and AI is a big part of that job right now. This site exists because the people closest to me, nurses, teachers, friends who've never touched a line of code, kept asking me the same question: "where do I even start?" I didn't have anywhere good to send them. So I built one.

Why this, why now

There's no shortage of content explaining what AI can theoretically do. What's missing is something written for people who don't work in tech, that starts from real life rather than features. I asked 100+ people what stops them using AI. Almost all of them already know AI exists and have used it for basic chats, but they still call themselves beginners.

The fear wasn't "I don't get it." It was "this feels too technical for me."

That's the gap this site is for. It's also my actual day job, not a side project. I've spent 10+ years building products people use, most recently taking an AI-powered analytics tool from nothing to a real product with thousands of active users.

Plenty of tech content about AI already exists. Almost none of it admits how simple most of this really is. People are overcomplicating it for beginners, and that's exactly what I'm trying not to do here.
