The person behind this site

Hi, I'm Soph.

I'm a product manager. Building things people actually use is my day job, not a side interest, and AI is a big part of that job right now. This site exists because the people closest to me, nurses, teachers, friends who've never touched a line of code, kept asking me the same question: "where do I even start?" I didn't have anywhere good to send them. So I built one.

Why this, why now

There's no shortage of content explaining what AI can theoretically do. What's missing is something written for people who don't work in tech, that starts from real life rather than features. I asked 48 people what actually stops them using AI. Almost all of them already use it for something and still call themselves a beginner. The fear wasn't "I don't get it," it was "this feels too technical for me." That's the gap this site is for.

Day job

I do this for real, not for fun

I've spent 8+ years building products people use, most recently taking an AI-powered analytics tool from nothing to a real product with thousands of active users.

Why I care

I got tired of the gap

Plenty of tech-world writing about AI. Almost none of it aimed at people who just want a straight answer for their actual life, not a demo.

What this isn't

Not a sales pitch

Nothing here is sponsored. I'm not trying to sell you a tool. I'm trying to save you the hours I've already spent figuring this stuff out.

Curious about the professional side of what I do, the actual product work, case studies and results? That lives on my full portfolio at sbizley.github.io. This site is the plain-English version, built for a different audience entirely.
