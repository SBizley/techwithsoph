You're not behind. Most people feel exactly like you.

I asked 48 people what actually stops them using AI. Almost all of them already use ChatGPT for something, but call themselves a beginner. The biggest fear wasn't "I don't get AI," it was "this feels too technical for me." That's the gap this site exists to close.

The real barrier

It's not about understanding AI

It's about confidence to actually try something with it. You don't need to understand how it works to get real use out of it, same as a car.

My one piece of advice

Automate one real task

Not ten. One. Pick something you already do by hand, a trip you're planning, a budget you keep meaning to sort, and start there.
