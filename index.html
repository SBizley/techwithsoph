I asked 100+ people what actually stops them using AI. Almost all of them already know AI exists and have used it for basic chats, but they still call themselves beginners.

You don't need to understand much about how it works to get real value out of it. My one piece of advice: automate one real task, not ten. Pick something you already do by hand, a trip you're planning, a budget you keep meaning to sort, and start there.
